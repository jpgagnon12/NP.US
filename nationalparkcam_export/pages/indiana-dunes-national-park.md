# Indiana Dunes National Park Guide

Source: https://www.national-parks.us/indiana-dunes-national-park

Indiana Dunes National Park is a beach, hiking, birding, and biodiversity destination close to Chicago and northern Indiana communities.

Introduction

Indiana Dunes protects Lake Michigan beaches, dunes, wetlands, oak savannas, prairies, and historic sites along the southern shore of the lake.

Planning Highlights

Visit West Beach, Mount Baldy, Cowles Bog, the Bailly Homestead and Chellberg Farm, and trail systems that cross dunes, forest, and wetland.

Camping and Lodging

Dunewood Campground is the main park campground. Nearby state park and regional lodging options add more choices during beach season.

Official Resources

Use the official NPS page, park map, current alerts, and reservation links below when planning a trip to Indiana Dunes National Park.
