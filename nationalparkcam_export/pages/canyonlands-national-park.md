# Canyonlands National Park Guide

Source: https://www.national-parks.us/canyonlands-national-park

Canyonlands National Park is divided into Island in the Sky, The Needles, The Maze, and the rivers, with each district requiring separate planning and travel time.

Introduction

Canyonlands preserves a maze of canyons, mesas, buttes, arches, and river corridors carved by the Colorado River and Green River.

Planning Highlights

Mesa Arch, Grand View Point, Upheaval Dome, Chesler Park, Elephant Hill, and river trips are signature experiences, but distances between districts are long.

Camping and Lodging

Island in the Sky and The Needles have frontcountry campgrounds, while many backcountry routes require permits. Four-wheel-drive roads demand high-clearance vehicles and careful preparation.

Official Resources

Use the official NPS page, park map, current alerts, and reservation links below when planning a trip to Canyonlands National Park.
