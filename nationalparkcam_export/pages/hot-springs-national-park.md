# Hot Springs National Park Guide

Source: https://www.national-parks.us/hot-springs-national-park

Hot Springs National Park blends urban access with historic bathhouses, mountain trails, scenic drives, and thermal water features in Arkansas.

Introduction

Hot Springs protects thermal springs, Bathhouse Row, forested Ouachita Mountain trails, and a long history of public bathing and wellness tourism.

Planning Highlights

Walk Bathhouse Row, tour the Fordyce Bathhouse Visitor Center, hike Hot Springs Mountain trails, and fill water bottles at public thermal fountains.

Camping and Lodging

Gulpha Gorge Campground offers sites inside the park. The campground is close to trails and downtown Hot Springs but can fill during busy periods.

Official Resources

Use the official NPS page, park map, current alerts, and reservation links below when planning a trip to Hot Springs National Park.
