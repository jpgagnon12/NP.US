Great Basin National Park offers high-elevation Nevada scenery, cave tours, night skies, and mountain hikes far from major crowds.

Introduction

Great Basin protects ancient bristlecone pines, Lehman Caves, dark night skies, alpine lakes, sagebrush basins, and Wheeler Peak.

Planning Highlights

Reserve a Lehman Caves tour, drive Wheeler Peak Scenic Drive seasonally, hike to bristlecone pines or alpine lakes, and stay for stargazing.

Camping and Lodging

Developed campgrounds sit at several elevations and seasons vary. Backcountry camping is available with low-impact travel practices.
