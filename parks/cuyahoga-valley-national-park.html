Cuyahoga Valley National Park is an accessible Ohio park built around trails, river scenery, historic canal features, and the Cuyahoga Valley Scenic Railroad.

Introduction

Cuyahoga Valley follows the Cuyahoga River between Cleveland and Akron, protecting forests, farms, waterfalls, wetlands, and canal history.

Planning Highlights

Visit Brandywine Falls, hike the Ledges Trail, bike or walk the Towpath Trail, and connect park stops with nearby gateway towns.

Camping and Lodging

There is no NPS campground in the park. Visitors usually stay in nearby communities or use regional campgrounds outside the park boundary.
